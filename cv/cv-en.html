Profile

I take AI and new ideas from prototype to production, including inside large, regulated organisations. Today I’m Product Lead at PagoPA, the state-controlled company behind Italy’s public digital payments and services platforms, where we launched the Public Administration’s first developer portal and an AI assistant that grew into an open-source RAG platform. Before that I co-founded six companies across Italy and the United States, with two exits. Every chapter started with a leap — from student to entrepreneur, from consulting to software products, from Milan to San Francisco, from entrepreneur to intrapreneur. And I still build hands-on: AI agents, local-first computer vision, and research on artificial valence.

How I work

  • Zero to oneAt my best when a product doesn’t exist yet; my ground is 0→1 more than scale-up.
  • Trust first, then autonomyEspecially with AI: privacy by design, autonomy only where it is reliable.
  • Remove friction, don’t add interfacesThe best technology is the kind you don’t see.
  • Quick, not dirtyEvery step becomes solid ground for the next; prototype before building the real product.
  • Build to understandClose to the code, ideas tested against real-world constraints.
  • Small teams, clear ownershipTwo-pizza teams, cross-pollination, proactive people; no diffused responsibility.
