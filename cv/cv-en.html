Profile

Over 25 years in tech: Product Lead at PagoPA, the state-controlled company behind Italy’s public digital payments and services platforms, after co-founding and leading six companies across Italy and the United States. Every chapter started with a leap — from student to entrepreneur, from consulting to software products, from Milan to San Francisco, from entrepreneur to intrapreneur. A computer scientist who still builds hands-on: AI agents, local-first computer vision, and research on artificial valence.

How I work

  • Zero to oneAt my best when a product doesn’t exist yet; my ground is 0→1 more than scale-up.
  • The best technology is the kind you don’t seeA good product removes friction instead of adding interfaces.
  • Quick, not dirtyEvery step becomes solid ground for the next; prototype before building the real product.
  • Build to understandClose to the code, ideas tested against real-world constraints.
  • Trust first, then autonomyEspecially with AI: privacy by design, autonomy only where it is reliable.
  • Small teams, clear ownershipTwo-pizza teams, cross-pollination, proactive people; no diffused responsibility.
